# Changelog

User-facing changes to [90minwaffle.co.uk](https://90minwaffle.co.uk), newest first. Live version: https://90minwaffle.co.uk/changelog/

## 2026-11-06

- Matches: the page heading and today's game count now show straight away, and the competition lists no longer jump about while the page loads.
- Privacy policy updated: how we spot people creating lots of accounts to game the leaderboard, and a 90-day limit on those records.

## 2026-11-01

- Live scores: stoppage time now shows as 45+ or 90+, and a game that stops updating (for example an abandoned match) no longer stays listed as live.
- Matches: live scores and minutes on the match cards now keep updating every 30 seconds while the page is open.
- Players in the menu now opens player search straight away, with the search box ready.
- Cleaner stats: clubs that appeared under two spellings in Turkey, Belgium, Greece, the Bundesliga, League One, League Two, Segunda and Ligue 2 are now merged, so form and head-to-head no longer count some games twice.

## 2026-10-31

- Matches: one game count everywhere on the page, finished games stay listed with their final score, and Live by competition shows the leagues being played.
- Home: when no top-league games are live, the live box says so and shows the next big kick-off.
- New page: How 90minWaffle works - where the data comes from, how forecasts and tips are made, and how every result is graded.
- New visitors can pick from popular clubs and countries, and Follow your club now opens right where you are.
- Tidier pages: clubs, players, countries, leagues, match previews, Previews, Analysis, FootballIQ, Tournaments, Me, Weekly reports and About now use tabs and dropdowns instead of one long scroll.
- News shows 12 stories then Show more, and the home page links straight to News.
- The Data still filling in label now disappears once a page's data is complete.
- Accuracy fixes: BTTS rates, the live HT label, last 5 results in previews, and rankings refreshing after each result. Each one is in the Corrections log.
- Leaderboard now counts players with 3 or more predictions.
- Live scores: finished games and games that have not started no longer show as live, and the live box always shows the minute, HT or LIVE.
- League pages now update every hour with accurate stats for each league's own season. Eredivisie no longer mixes in Conference League games.
- Danish Superliga stats are back with every game this season, and MLS and several other leagues now count far more games.
- Matches and Me pages no longer jump around while they load.
- League page titles are shorter so they show in full on Google.
- Edges: new 'How sure are we?' box - shows honestly whether our record is beyond luck yet, and how many more tips it would take.

## Earlier in October 2026

- **Homepage redesign:** calmer navy palette, wider desktop layout, and a "Your team" strip when you follow a club.
- **Notifications:** cleaner text, one icon, and tapping a notification opens the exact match or page.
- **Match previews:** one prediction card per match; previews you have read no longer reappear as new; links to related previews and each club's next match; rescheduled fixtures handled cleanly.
- **FootballIQ rankings:** rebuilt from cleaned results; top 10 with "Show all"; tapping a team opens its panel and links straight to the matching edge.
- **Menus:** simpler phone and desktop menus.
- **Tooltips:** plain-English explanations for more terms, and corrected wording for edges and ROI.
- **Search data:** match pages now give search engines complete event details.
- **Security and reliability:** the site now only accepts traffic through its CDN; automatic certificate renewal fixed; key pages are checked every two minutes and restored automatically if an update ever goes wrong; unused credentials removed.
